World City Bus

Simulator · 12 cities, 12 legendary buses

Take the wheel of a real city bus! Drive 12 legendary buses through 12 famous cities, from San Francisco and Vancouver to Zürich, Lyon, Athens and Beijing, and get your passengers there on time.

Get it on Google Play

Free · Android

What is inside

  • A real city bus simulator Accelerate and brake gently, keep to the speed limits and slow down before every curve. Take a bend too fast and the bus rolls over. Stop right at the stop pole, open the doors and watch passengers board and take their seats.
  • Avenues, roadworks and accidents Long dual-carriageway avenues lead out to new housing estates. Overtake slower cars and change lanes in time when roadworks or an accident block your lane. Express lines take you across the whole city.
  • Trams in every city Trams run on their own tracks through the streets. Share the road with them and give way at the crossings.
  • 12 cities, 133 runs Gdynia, San Francisco, Salzburg, Milan, Vancouver, Valparaíso, Zürich, Lyon, Bern, Athens, Seattle and Beijing.
  • 12 legendary buses A London double-decker with an open platform, an American “fishbowl”, a Hungarian articulated legend, a Swedish 19-metre BRT articulated, a Chinese electric pioneer and more, each with its own cab and dashboard.

Free to play

The whole game is free and shows ads: banners on the menu screens and the run summary, a small banner while the doors are open at a stop, an occasional full-screen ad and optional rewarded videos.

Two one-time purchases: No Ads removes all ads; the Full Version removes all ads and unlocks every city and bus. No subscription.

No account needed. Languages: English, Polish, German, Czech, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Japanese.
