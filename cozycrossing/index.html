Cozy Railway Crossing

Open and close the barrier, keep the trains safe

Be the keeper of a little railway crossing in the countryside, by the sea and in town. A relaxing two-button game: the left button closes the barrier, the right one opens it. Let the cars through and close it before the train arrives.

What is inside

  • Two buttons, one minute Let the cars through and close the barrier before a train arrives — a sign by the track shows where the next train is coming from and how many seconds are left. Close too late and a car bumps into the train: no worries, just try again.
  • 30 cozy places A meadow, a wheat field with a combine harvester, a village, an orchard, a forest, a lake, a vineyard, a lavender field, a harbour, the mountains, the seaside, a sawmill, a station, downtown and a busy rail junction — by day, at night, at dawn and at dusk, each with its own season.
  • More and more trains Start with a single track and work up to four, then two crossings one after another. Passenger trains, steam locomotives, railbuses, express trains, trams and long freight trains carrying containers, logs, tractors, sheep, pumpkins and Christmas trees.
  • Cyclists and scooters They hurry across too, and get really grumpy if a train whooshes past right in front of them. On two levels a cycling race goes by — let the peloton and the rider in the yellow jersey through.
  • Little details The keeper's walk to the hut, animals running across the fields and calm, gentle music in the background.

Free, with ads

The game is free and shows full-screen ads: after a passed level, and if you get stuck on a level, at most about once every 2 minutes. A one-time purchase removes all ads. All 30 levels are free.

No account needed. Languages: English, Polish, German, Czech, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Japanese.
