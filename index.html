AirFry

Air fryer recipes and timer

More than a hundred recipes with temperature, time and ingredients — and a timer that tells you when to turn the food, shake the basket or add the cheese. Recipes, timer, calculator and shopping list all work with no internet.

Coming soon to Google Play

Android 8.0 or newer.

What is inside

  • 100+ recipes Meat, fish, vegetables, potatoes, bakes, breakfast, snacks, desserts and reheating. Each with temperature, time, ingredients and steps.
  • A timer that tells you what to do It reminds you to turn the food and shake the basket partway through. It rings at alarm volume, so you hear it over running water, and keeps counting after you leave the app.
  • Oven-to-air-fryer calculator The packaging only gives oven settings? Enter its temperature and time and get the air fryer equivalent.
  • A shopping list that merges Two recipes with onion give you one line, not two. Ticked items drop to the bottom so you can see what is already in the basket.
  • Weekly plan Assign recipes to days, then turn the whole week into a single shopping list with one tap.
  • Nine languages, two temperature scales English, Polish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch and Chinese — the ingredients, steps and tips are translated too, not just the interface. Celsius or Fahrenheit.
  • Your own recipes and notes Add your own, write a note on a built-in recipe as well, mark favourites and group them into collections.
  • Works offline Recipes, the timer, the calculator and the shopping list all work in aeroplane mode. No account, no sign-in.

Privacy, without asterisks

The app has no server and never sends anywhere what you cook. Your recipes, notes, shopping list and weekly plan stay on your phone — there is no account, no sign-in and no analytics.

The internet is needed only for the ads that pay for the app. If you would rather not see them, a one-time purchase removes them from every screen. No subscription.
