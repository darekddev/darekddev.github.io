What is inside

  • Drive, switch, deliver Throw the switches ahead of your train and pick the right track: a siding, the platform track or the passing loop. Couple tank wagons, load glass and leave the wagons in the right zone.
  • Signals and timetables Pass a red signal and you risk a head-on collision. Arrive on time, stop exactly at the platform and let the passengers board.
  • Every cargo has its rules Smooth rides for passengers, 60 km/h for tank wagons, a race against the clock for reefers, no jolts for glass. Take a curve too fast and the train can roll over.
  • 12 routes across Europe Kraków, Gdańsk, Berlin, Praha, Budapest, Milano, Paris, Lisboa, Madrid, London and the Paris–Lyon high-speed line at 300 km/h — with real station names and single-track lines.
  • Six locomotives, three views From the Bulldog shunter to the Meteor high-speed train and the Duchess steam engine. Chase camera, top view or the driver's seat with live gauges.
  • Stars and coins Earn up to 3 stars per mission to open new routes and buy locomotives in the depot. 90 missions, no account needed, in 8 languages.

Free, with ads

The game is free and shows ads: banners in the menus and on the mission summary, a small banner at the top while passengers board or cargo is loaded, an occasional full-screen ad after a mission and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. A one-time Full Version removes all ads and unlocks every locomotive.
