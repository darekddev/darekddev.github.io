Metro Leap

Rush Hour · hold, release, leap!

Rush hour in the big city — and you are late! Hold your finger to charge the dash, release to leap over cones, bikes, crowds, ticket gates and the gap between platform and train. Land on the glowing spots for PERFECT jumps.

What is inside

  • One-touch running Hold to charge, release to leap. PERFECT landings build a combo multiplier up to x5 — hold too long and you OVERLOAD.
  • Four stages, four bosses City streets at sunset, the metro, the neon Neo Metro and Neon City. Every stage ends with a boss chase: fire, lightning, laser hurdles and plasma.
  • Endless laps Finished all four stages? The city loops — and every lap is faster.
  • 9 runners Sumo, Granny, Tourist, Businessman, Salaryman, Ramen Chef, Shiba, Ninja and the Legend — unlock them by running further. Free Daily Box every day.

Free to play

The whole game is free. A banner appears on the end-of-run screen and in the pause menu — never during a run. Optional rewarded ads give you +1 life or a second daily box.

One small purchase, the Full Version, removes the ads and unlocks the Legend runner. One payment, no subscription.

No account and no chat; plays offline. Languages: English, German, Polish, Spanish, Portuguese and Japanese.
