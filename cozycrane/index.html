Cozy Crane: Build Puzzle

Operate a friendly crane and build a cozy town

Take the controls of a friendly tower crane and build a whole cozy town, one big block at a time. A relaxing building puzzle with step-by-step instructions, just like a box of toy bricks.

What is inside

  • Drag, lift and click Drag your finger and the crane turns, the trolley rolls and the hook follows you. Let go and the hook goes down — the magnet grabs the glowing block. Carry it over the glowing hologram, let go — click! A small red cross marks the exact spot.
  • 30 charming buildings A pizzeria, a pastel bakery, an ice cream parlor with a giant cone, a sushi bar, a fire station, a police station, a clinic, a lighthouse on the beach, a windmill, a retro cinema, a toy store, an observatory, an alpine cabin, a Dutch canal house, a clock tower, a castle, a cozy hotel with a rooftop pool and more.
  • The town comes alive When the last block clicks into place, confetti flies and smoke curls from the chimneys. Then the building opens: guests sit at the tables, people walk around, a fire engine, a police car and an ambulance drive up with flashing lights.
  • Cozy details A little train rolls past, a taxi drops off curious passers-by and a truck delivers new blocks. Soft pastel colours, rounded blocks and calm music — no timers, no stress.

Free, with ads

The game is free and shows a full-screen ad only after a finished building — never while you build. A one-time purchase unlocks all buildings at once and removes all ads. Without it, the buildings unlock one by one for free.

No account needed. Languages: English, Polish, German, Czech, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Japanese.
