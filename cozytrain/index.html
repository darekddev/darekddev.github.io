Cozy Train Simulator

A relaxing train ride from town to town

Blow the whistle, close the doors and roll gently through fields of wheat, lavender and sunflowers. No crashes, no game over and no timer — just you, a little locomotive and the people and parcels waiting at the next station.

What is inside

  • Passengers, freight and crane jobs Every route has its own task. Passengers wear the color and shape of their station; freight is stacked on the flatcars by hand; big loads — a tulip container, a fishing boat, a combine harvester, a giant teddy bear — go on with a tower crane.
  • Gentle driving Set the points and pick the right track to every station. Brake too hard and the driver's tea spills. On the next track other trains roll out of tunnels and their drivers wave to you.
  • 9 regions, 27 routes Masuria, Bavaria, the Netherlands, Tuscany, Provence, Portugal, New England, Japan and Switzerland — little towns on both sides of the track and real station names in every region.
  • A lively, cozy world A combine harvester cuts the wheat, sheep graze on the tracks, kids run with kites, geese fly over the fields and lanterns rise at dusk. Spring, summer, autumn or winter, from sunrise to the evening lamps.
  • Your engine shed and home Seven locomotives, six cute animal themes, paint, wagon decorations, the driver's hat, a cab pet, upgrades and twelve horns — and a home to furnish after your shift.
  • No timer, just rides Every ride pays right away: the fare for the distance plus a tip for a gentle ride. Every ride brings new routes closer. Easy driving mode and a short tutorial help new drivers.

Free, with ads

The game is free and shows ads: a short full-screen ad roughly every minute and a half, always while the train is stopped, and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (double coins). A one-time Full Version removes all ads, opens every route and unlocks the whole engine shed shop.

No account needed. Languages: English, Polish, German, Czech, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Japanese.
