What is inside

  • A real trolleybus simulator Accelerate and brake with the power controller, keep to the speed limits and take curves gently — your passengers feel every jolt.
  • Keep to the wires Slow down through the overhead switches — too fast and the poles jump off the wires. Modern trolleybuses carry a battery for streets without wires; vintage ones stay under the wires.
  • Change your route Streets get closed for roadworks and the navigation shows a detour. Pause the game to see the whole map with the line, every stop and your route.
  • 12 cities, 85 runs Gdynia, San Francisco, Salzburg, Milan, Vancouver, Valparaíso, Zürich, Lyon, Bern, Athens, Seattle and Beijing — diagonal avenues, ring roads, rivers with bridges and famous landmarks.
  • 15 trolleybuses, three views From a 1946 veteran and a London double-decker to a Hungarian articulated bus and a modern Alpine articulated, each with its own cab. Drive from the cab and look around, or watch from behind or from the side as passengers take their seats.
  • Stars, coins and radio Every run is rated for punctuality, stopping accuracy, comfort, safety and route. Earn stars for new cities and coins for new trolleybuses. No account needed, in 9 languages.

Free, with ads

The game is free and shows ads: banners on the city and run screens and on the summary, a small banner at the top while the doors are open at a stop, an occasional full-screen ad at a stop or after a run and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. A one-time Full Version removes all ads and unlocks every city and trolleybus.
