Shonen Soccer: Legends

A manga football career for one player — yours

Create your footballer, start on a village dirt pitch and climb all the way to the top league — with fire shots, Super Shots, Powers and a whole wardrobe of gear along the way. All offline, no account.

What is inside

  • Your player, your look Pick a skin tone, a hairstyle (11 of them, including the spiky Legend cut), a beard and a hair colour. Later the barber changes it all for coins.
  • From the dirt pitch to the top tier 28 leagues in 13 countries, five tiers each. Win the league to go up, avoid the bottom to stay up and accept offers from bigger clubs between seasons. The lower tiers play on mud and sand; the grass comes with promotion.
  • One player on the pitch Eleven-a-side matches, but you control only your footballer in his position: defence, midfield or attack. The coach sets tasks before each match, and his trust decides whether you start, come off the bench or watch from the stands.
  • Powers and Super Shots Unlock 14 Powers such as Comet Slide, Black Hole, Tidal Wave or Time Stop, the Fire Shot fired with a fast swipe and four Super Shots: Meteor Drop, Super Comet, Ice Spear and Shockwave Cannon.
  • 50 items of gear Boots, shirts, shorts, caps, headbands and glasses — from torn sneakers to golden Legend gear. Each gives a small bonus, and the best ones help your whole team. Plus celebrations and match boosts.
  • Training and 7 languages Nine short training drills between matches give you form for the next one. English, Polish, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese (Brazil) and Japanese.

Free, with ads

The game is free and shows ads: a banner on the pause screen and optional rewarded videos for extras such as a second daily box, double match rewards or an extra training. A single optional purchase — the Full version — turns the ads off for good and gives every one of those extras instantly, including free skill tree resets.
