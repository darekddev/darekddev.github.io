Shonen Soccer: Striker

Manga football career

Become the striker everyone talks about. Every goal is drawn with your finger — from a concrete pitch behind your school, through five leagues and the European Cup, all the way to the World Tournament with your national team.

What is inside

  • Swipe to shoot One-on-ones, rebounds, headers, volleys, bicycle kicks and free kicks over the wall.
  • From the school pitch to the top Start at 14, climb through the academy and five English leagues, take transfer offers and play in the European Cup.
  • Your player card Sniper, Target Man, Technician or Sprinter — grow your card with evolutions and training mini-games.
  • A real career Match ratings, sponsors, rivals for your place, injuries, awards, a trophy cabinet and a season-by-season bio.
  • World Tournament Lead your national team to world glory.
  • No account needed Your career is saved on your phone.

Free, with ads

The game is free and shows ads: a banner on the match and drill result screens of your career, and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch.
