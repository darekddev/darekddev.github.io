Tickets, Please!

Train Tycoon · a cozy conductor game

All aboard! You are the conductor of a little regional train, and every station brings new passengers, new requests and a bit of chaos to sort out. Check tickets, serve coffee, keep everyone comfy — and turn your train into the coziest ride in the country.

What is inside

  • On board Check tickets: the date, the destination, discounts without an ID, second-class tickets in first class. Valid, fine or sell a new one — you decide. Bring coffee and sandwiches, mop up spills, unblock the toilet, find a lost child and chase the occasional fare dodger.
  • Your dream train Earn coins and prestige and open new stations on an endless loop. Buy new cars — open saloon, compartments, first class, dining, sleeper, panorama, family and a club car with a piano — and rearrange them by dragging in the depot.
  • Cozy interiors Furnish every car on a grid: swivel chairs facing the window, sofas, a crayon table for kids, a snack machine, a book-crossing shelf and much more. Paint the outside, choose wallpaper, curtains and floors.
  • Crew and upgrades Hire a crew to help you and upgrade your cars with air conditioning, screens and coffee machines.
  • Cozy world Day and night trips, four seasons, rain and snow, special seasonal guests, a sticker album, conductor uniforms, dogs travelling with their owners and calm music. A daily gift box rewards you for coming back.

Free to play

The whole game is free and coins are earned only by playing. A small banner appears on the end-of-day summary and in the pause menu, and a full-screen ad may show when you leave the summary — never while you ride. Rewarded ads (double earnings, an intern, a double daily gift) are always your choice.

One small purchase, No Ads, removes banner and full-screen ads and includes the Robot conductor uniform. One payment, no subscription.

No account and no chat; plays offline. Languages: English, Polish, German, Czech, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Japanese.
