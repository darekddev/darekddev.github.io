What is inside

  • A real tram simulator Accelerate and brake with the power lever, keep to the speed limits and take curves gently — your passengers feel every jolt.
  • Stop on the mark Stop precisely at the T mark: the closer you get, the bigger the bonus. Open the doors, wait for the passengers, close the doors and move on.
  • Bells, signals and switches Ring the bell for pedestrians, obey the tram signals, watch for cars at junctions, set the switches into the depot and turn around on the terminal loop.
  • 12 cities, 60 runs Melbourne, Amsterdam, Vienna, Prague, Kraków, Budapest, Milan, Istanbul, Hong Kong, Lisbon, San Francisco and Tokyo — real stop names, landmarks, rivers and bridges, traffic and other trams.
  • 12 iconic trams, five views From Lisbon's little yellow tram to a Hong Kong double-decker, each with its own interior and cab. Drive from the cab or watch from behind, from an angle, from the side or from above.
  • Stars, coins and radio Every run is rated for punctuality, stopping accuracy, comfort and safety. Earn stars for new cities and coins for new trams. No account needed, in 9 languages.

Free, with ads

The game is free and shows ads: banners on the city and run screens and on the summary, a small banner at the top while the tram stands at a stop with the doors open, an occasional full-screen ad at the loop or after a run and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. A one-time Full Version removes all ads and unlocks every city and tram.
