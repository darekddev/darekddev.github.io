Cozy Tram Simulator

A relaxing tram ride through pastel cities

Ring the bell, close the doors and roll gently through a pastel little town. No crashes and no game over — just you, your tram and passengers who want to get home.

What is inside

  • Drive at your own pace Set the switches ahead of the tram and pick the right track to every stop. Open the doors at the platform, let everyone board and ring the bell before you leave.
  • Passengers and special guests Passengers wear the color and shape of their stop. Guests ask for small favors: grandma wants a gentle ride, a tourist wants the scenic detour, a little boy wants you to ring the bell.
  • Cities around the world Wrocław, Prague, Paris, Rome, Lisbon, San Francisco and Tokyo — cobbled old towns, river quays, hills, cable cars, neon streets and cherry trees. More cities are on the way.
  • Every season, day and night Spring, summer, autumn or winter for any district, by day or on the night shift. Falling leaves and puddles, snowmen on the tracks, kids throwing snowballs and Santa with his sack.
  • A lively town Ducks nap on the rails, a busker plays by the passing loop, kids wave from the pavement and people sip coffee at little cafés. Ring the bell and the town answers.
  • Your own depot Wash your tram with a sponge, a hose or the automatic car wash, pet the depot cat and dog, and spend coins on new trams, paint, decorations, hats and bells.

Free, with ads

The game is free and shows ads: small banners on the city and district maps and on the ride summary, and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (double coins). A one-time Full Version removes all ads and unlocks the whole depot shop.

No account needed. Languages: English, Polish, German, Czech, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Japanese.
